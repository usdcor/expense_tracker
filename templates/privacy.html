Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect your name, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password. As you use the Service, we also store the expenses you log — including amount, category, date, and any description you add. We do not collect payment card numbers, bank credentials, or other sensitive financial account details.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide solely to operate the Service: authenticating your account, displaying your expenses back to you, and generating the summaries and filters you request. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in a database associated with your account and are accessible only when you are signed in. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed before being saved. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. Where the Service relies on third-party infrastructure (such as hosting or font delivery), those providers only process the minimum data necessary to deliver the Service and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, or if you would like to request access to or deletion of your data, please reach out through your account settings or contact the Spendly team directly.
